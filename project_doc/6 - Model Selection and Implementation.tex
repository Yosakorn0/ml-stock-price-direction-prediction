\chapter{MODEL IMPLEMENTATION}

\section{Model Selection: XGBoost vs. Baseline Ensembles}
The primary model selected for this system is the \textbf{XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting)} classifier. XGBoost was chosen over standard Gradient Boosting or deep learning (LSTM) due to its unique ability to handle tabular financial data with sparse NLP features. Theoretically, XGBoost addresses the non-linear dynamics of market volatility \citep{chen2016} by utilizing an ensemble of shallow decision trees to minimize a second-order Taylor expansion of the loss function.

\section{Hyperparameter Optimization and Tuning}
To ensure model robustness, we utilized an automated randomized search for hyperparameter optimization. The objective was to minimize logarithmic loss while maximizing the accuracy across all five asset classes; the detailed search space and the optimal configuration selected for the final nodes are documented in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:hyperparameters}.

\section{Bias Analysis and Testing}
A rigorous scientific process requires the identification and mitigation of model bias.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Look-ahead Bias}: To prevent this, we enforcement a strict temporal split. The model is forbidden from seeing $T+1$ news or market data during the training phase at time $T$.
    \item \textbf{Selection Bias}: We mitigate this by applying the pipeline to disparate asset classes (Equities, Commodities, Crypto) to ensure the features (RSI/Sentiment) are globally relevant and not over-fit to a single market regime.
    \item \textbf{Survivor Bias}: The dataset utilizes all provided tickers regardless of past performance during the 2020-2026 window.
\end{itemize}

\section{System Trust and Validation}
Scientifically, "Trust" is established through the consistency of SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) values and walk-forward verification. We observe that sentiment features contribute to a 12--15\% relative importance in predicting directionality, which provides a numerical foundation for the behavioral finance hypothesis.
