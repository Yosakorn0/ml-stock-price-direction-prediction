\chapter{MODEL EVALUATION}

\section{Evaluation Strategy: Multi-Asset Performance}
Model performance is assessed across MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, Gold, and Bitcoin datasets using time-series walk-forward validation. We compare two distinct configurations to scientifically measure the contribution of sentiment polarity:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Technical Baseline}: Using only OHLCV and technical indicators (RSI, MACD).
    \item \textbf{Multi-Source Hybrid}: Integrating Technical, Macro (FRED), and FinBERT Sentiment.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scientific Performance Metrics}
The comparative analysis of accuracy and the corresponding F1-scores is presented in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:model_comparison}. We observe a consistent 3--5\% performance gain when behavioral sentiment is integrated, suggesting that news-driven signals provide a non-redundant predictive alpha.

\section{Trust and Risk Analysis: Sharpe Ratios}
To establish the "Scientific Trust" of the model beyond simple accuracy, we calculate the **Annualized Sharpe Ratio** based on a simulated directional strategy. A Sharpe ratio $> 1.0$ is generally considered excellent for algorithmic trading; our model's performance relative to the Buy \& Hold baseline is detailed in Appendix Table~\ref{tab:sharpe_ratios}, confirming the successful capture of risk-adjusted momentum.
