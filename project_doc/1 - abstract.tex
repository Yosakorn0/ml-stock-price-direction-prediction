\begin{abstract}
    Predicting the directional movement of financial assets remains a foundational challenge in quantitative finance due to the non-stationarity and high-entropy nature of market data. This research presents a \textbf{Multi-Source Financial Intelligence System} that integrates heterogeneous data streams---including historical price action, macroeconomic indicators, and transformer-based sentiment analysis---to mitigate information asymmetry. Utilizing \textbf{FinBERT}, a domain-specific pre-trained transformer, we extract daily sentiment polarity from news headlines, which serves as a proxy for behavioral market drivers. The modeling pipeline employs an \textbf{XGBoost} ensemble, scientifically validated through Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) tests to address non-stationarity and time-series walk-forward validation to ensure robustness. Our results demonstrate that the fusion of behavioral sentiment with technical indicators provides a statistically significant edge over random-walk baselines, achieving annualized Sharpe Ratios exceeding 5.5 across diversified asset classes (Equities, Gold, and Crypto). This study provides a scientific framework for trust-based machine learning in stochastic environments, bridging the gap between quantitative momentum and behavioral finance.
\end{abstract}
